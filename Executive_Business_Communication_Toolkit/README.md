## 🎯 The Challenge

Executive offices send dozens of formal emails a day. Inconsistent tone and structure slow responses and weaken the office's voice.

## ✅ The Solution

A Word toolkit of professionally structured email templates for executive office, CEO correspondence, PMO coordination, HR, vendors and clients.

<p align="center"><img src="Executive_Business_Communication_Toolkit_Preview.png" width="100%" alt="Executive Business Communication Toolkit preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Executive & CEO office** | Approvals, meeting invitations and leadership updates |
| **Project & PMO** | Coordination, follow-ups and status requests |
| **HR & employees** | Notifications and administrative communication |
| **Vendors & clients** | Formal external correspondence |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Consistent, professional executive tone
- ✅ Subject lines and structure ready to reuse
- ✅ Covers internal and external audiences
- ✅ Saves drafting time every day
- ✅ Editable Word file plus PDF
