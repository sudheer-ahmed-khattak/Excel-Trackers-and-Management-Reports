## 🎯 The Challenge

Senior leaders do not read spreadsheets in meetings. They need the position, the risks and the decisions required – on a handful of well-designed slides.

## ✅ The Solution

An 8-slide executive deck that shows how operational data becomes a KPI pack, how the board meeting cycle runs, and how documents, procurement and payments are reported to leadership.

<p align="center"><img src="Executive_Management_Board_Support_Preview.png" width="100%" alt="Executive Management Presentation preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Slides | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Slides 1–2** | Executive reporting & board support – positioning |
| **Slide 3** | Reporting operating model: raw inputs → decisions → closed actions |
| **Slide 4** | Sample executive KPI pack: status, risk and action closure |
| **Slide 5** | Board & management meeting cycle: agenda, minutes, approvals, actions |
| **Slides 6–7** | Document control reporting and procurement / payment control |
| **Slide 8** | Roadmap – how the reporting outputs map to executive roles |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Repeatable reporting flow from data to decision
- ✅ KPI pack combining status, risk and action closure
- ✅ Board meeting cycle from agenda to closed actions
- ✅ Clean, consistent executive design language
- ✅ Editable PowerPoint plus a print-ready PDF
