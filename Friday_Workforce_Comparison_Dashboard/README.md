## 🎯 The Challenge

Weekly manpower reports were compared by eye. Additions and removals were missed, and department variances could not be explained to management.

## ✅ The Solution

An Excel comparison report that matches two Friday workforce lists and shows added and removed employees, variance by department and location, and the net movement.

<p align="center"><img src="Friday_Workforce_Comparison_Dashboard_Preview.png" width="100%" alt="Friday Workforce Comparison Dashboard preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Sheet / Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Old Friday** | Previous week workforce list |
| **Current Friday** | This week workforce list |
| **Comparison Report** | Added / removed staff, variance by department and location, KPI cards |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Automatic match of two weekly lists
- ✅ Added and removed employees listed by name
- ✅ Variance by department and by location
- ✅ Net movement KPI cards for management
- ✅ Repeatable every week – paste and refresh
