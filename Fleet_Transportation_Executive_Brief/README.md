## 🎯 The Challenge

Moving thousands of workers every day depends on vehicles, drivers and routes being ready. Breakdowns and gaps were discovered at the gate, not before.

## ✅ The Solution

An Excel control tower that brings the fleet register, driver and route plan and facilities services together, with availability and utilisation KPIs for the morning brief.

<p align="center"><img src="Fleet_Transportation_Executive_Brief.png" width="100%" alt="Fleet Transportation Executive Brief preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Sheet / Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Control Tower** | Fleet availability, utilisation, trips and status KPIs |
| **Fleet Register** | Vehicles with type, status and assignment |
| **Driver & Route Plan** | Driver allocation by route and shift |
| **Facilities Services** | Linked facilities service tracking |
| **Lists** | Drop-down sources |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Morning-brief KPIs for availability and utilisation
- ✅ Vehicle status and assignment in one register
- ✅ Driver and route plan by shift
- ✅ Facilities services linked to transport
- ✅ Ready to print for the daily operations meeting
