## 🎯 The Challenge

Decisions taken in management meetings were getting lost between minutes and inboxes. Nobody could say how many actions were open, who owned them or how long they had been waiting.

## ✅ The Solution

An Excel tracker with an executive dashboard that shows open, overdue and high-priority actions, ageing and owner performance – the same list the Executive Office prints for the CEO's daily agenda.

<p align="center"><img src="Management_Action_Tracker_Preview.png" width="100%" alt="Management Action Tracker preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Sheet / Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Executive Dashboard** | Status split, priority profile, ageing buckets, department follow-up and completion rate |
| **Action Tracker** | Action, owner, department, priority, due date, status and comments |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Ageing analysis shows exactly how long actions have been waiting
- ✅ Priority profile keeps the critical few in front of leadership
- ✅ Owner and department views drive accountability
- ✅ Filter open items and print – ready for the daily agenda
- ✅ Completion rate KPI to measure follow-up discipline
