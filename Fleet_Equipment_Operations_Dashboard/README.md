## Executive Summary

The **Fleet Equipment Operations Dashboard** is an executive reporting solution developed to provide project leadership with a centralized overview of fleet availability, equipment utilization, ownership status, operational readiness, deployment, and equipment performance across multiple project locations.

The dashboard transforms operational fleet data into actionable business insights, enabling management to optimize equipment utilization, monitor asset allocation, improve operational planning, and support strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Objective

This dashboard was developed to help management:

- Monitor fleet utilization
- Track equipment availability
- Review equipment ownership
- Monitor project-wise deployment
- Analyze equipment distribution
- Improve fleet planning
- Optimize operational resources
- Support executive decision-making
