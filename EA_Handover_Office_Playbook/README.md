# 📘 EA Handover & Office Playbook

![Preview](preview.png)

An 11-slide **PowerPoint handover pack** for the Office of a Project Director. It gives an incoming Executive Assistant everything needed to run the office from day one: routines, meetings, filing, drivers, travel, contacts, system access and a formal sign-off.

> ⚠️ **All data is fictional.** Names, companies, phone numbers and references are demo data.

## 📂 Files

## 🧭 What's inside
1. **Title**
2. **What this playbook covers**: 6 areas
3. **Daily routine**: hour-by-hour timeline plus the office's non-negotiables
4. **Weekly & monthly cycle**: Sunday–Thursday calendar and monthly deadlines
5. **Meeting management**: before, during and after
6. **Filing structure & naming rule**: shared-drive tree and file naming convention
7. **Drivers & travel**: driver roster and the 5-step travel process
8. **Key contacts**
9. **Systems & access transfer**: Outlook, Oracle, DocuSign, Aconex, keys and visitor system
10. **Handover checklist & sign-off**
11. **The first 30 days**: shadow → co-run → own

## 🛠️ Skills shown
Executive support · Diary & meeting management · Records management · Fleet & travel coordination · Knowledge transfer · Business continuity
