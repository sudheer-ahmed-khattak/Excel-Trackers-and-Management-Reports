## 🎯 The Challenge

With thousands of people across several sites, management could not see attendance trends, overtime cost or exceptions until month-end – too late to act.

## ✅ The Solution

An interactive Power BI dashboard on a structured Excel dataset that tracks attendance, absence, overtime hours, approval status and cost, with slicers for month, department and site.

<p align="center"><img src="Executive_Operations_Workforce_KPI_Dashboard_Preview.png" width="100%" alt="Executive Operations Workforce KPI Dashboard preview" /></p>

## 📦 What's Inside

| Component | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Power BI report** | KPI cards, attendance status, site distribution, overtime by department, monthly hours and cost trends |
| **Excel dataset** | Employees, Attendance, Overtime, Departments, Calendar and KPI Targets tables |
| **PDF report** | Print-ready version of the dashboard |

## ⭐ Key Features

- ✅ Attendance and absence rate at a glance
- ✅ Overtime hours and cost trend by month
- ✅ Approved vs pending overtime and exception count
- ✅ Interactive month, department and site filters
- ✅ Star-schema data model with Power Query and DAX
